whether you listen to mainstream artists, or even niche ones, music really is the shit when you're not feeling it at all, or just when you want to have a good time. I, for example, have probably the whitest music taste of all time, but to be honest im really happy about it because its music that helps me go through it, theres something about it that instantly makes me feel better, and i think it is magic.

so i really want to talk about my favourite songs (atleast by the time im writing this) and my favorite songs from them.

<h1 align="center"><a href="https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=qcvkKvMixS8">Steve Lacy - Mercury</a></h1>
<p align="center"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/d/de/Steve_Lacy_-_Gemini_Rights.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=original"></p>

**Mercury** is the 3rd track off of Steve Lacy's "Gemini Rights", aside from the whole album being lowkey a banger, this track is my personal favorite, i love the rhythm of this track but most importantly the little choirs you hear at the start, in the middle but most importantly [the part where the beat shuts off.](https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=qcvkKvMixS8&t=188)

I really like the guitar solo [on this part](https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=H9tuaWD1URo&t=101), and the little *"ohhh yeahhhhhhh"* voices in the background, they are so soothing for some reason, then after that comes 

But i think the best part of this song is [this part right here](https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=H9tuaWD1URo&t=127), the lil high voices in the background and the end of each verse make me float, not to mention the fucking ending, crazy shit if you ask me, i love the whole chord progression and that fuzzy synth at the end.

<h1 align="center"><a href="https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=m91Vq-Yd3BA">Tyler, The Creator - I THINK</a></h1>
<p align="center"><img src="https://media.pitchfork.com/photos/6454f9ce60430f8f9be0fb09/master/pass/Tyler-the-Creator-Igor.jpg" width="300" height="300"></p>

to be clear i have to put the entirety of igor here, because its my personal favorite album of all time (told yall my music taste white af), and its really hard to pick a favorite, but if they ask me my favorite song from the album with a gun pointed to my head, i'd instantly say **I THINK**.

this track has been my favorite from IGOR since ive first started listening to tyler, its so fucking groovy as a song, not to mention the bridge and the fucking piano at the end, crazy shit, breakup really had to hit that hard to make a song and album this phenomenal.
